Services · 03

Lawns & Decking

Comfortable surfaces help a garden feel ready for everyday life. We build decking and prepare natural or artificial lawns around the shape, levels and practical demands of your space.

Discuss your project ↗

01 · Outdoor living

Premium Decking

Decking can create a useful seating or entertaining area where levels, access or the shape of the garden make paving less suitable. We prepare the sub-base and framing before fitting boards, edges and steps with a clean finish.

The layout is planned around safe movement, useful proportions and the relationship with the home and garden.

02 · A fresh start

Turfing & Lawns

A good lawn depends on what sits beneath it. We prepare and level the ground, lay fresh turf with tidy joins and form considered edges around beds, paths and patios.

Once installed, practical establishment guidance helps the new lawn settle in and develop into a usable green space.

03 · Green all year

Artificial Grass

Artificial grass can suit busy gardens where a consistent, lower-maintenance surface is preferred. Installation includes excavation, a stable prepared base, suitable drainage and secure perimeter edges.

Careful cutting and neat joins are important to achieving a natural-looking finish that fits cleanly around the rest of the garden.
