Outdoor living

Premium Decking

Garden decking can create a practical space for seating, dining and entertaining, especially in gardens with uneven ground or changing levels. We install decking to suit the shape and layout of your outdoor space.

We carefully prepare the base and frame before fitting the decking boards, steps and edges. The result is a strong, safe and attractive deck designed for everyday use and built to last.

A fresh start

Turfing & Lawns

A healthy lawn starts with proper ground preparation. We level and prepare the soil before laying fresh turf with neat joins and clean edges around paths, patios and flower beds.

We make sure the new lawn is laid correctly and provide simple aftercare advice to help the turf settle, establish strong roots and grow into a neat, usable garden lawn.

Green all year

Artificial Grass

Artificial grass provides a neat, green lawn all year round with very little maintenance. It is a practical choice for busy gardens, family spaces and areas where natural grass is difficult to maintain.

We offer a range of artificial grass styles to create a soft, clean and natural-looking finish. The result is an attractive, durable lawn that stays tidy without regular mowing, watering or feeding.
