Space to use

Paving & Patios

A well-designed patio can create a practical outdoor space for relaxing, dining and entertaining. We provide patio installation with careful planning around layout, paths, seating areas, edging and drainage.

We prepare the ground properly and lay each patio on a strong sub-base for a durable, long-lasting finish. Paving materials, slab sizes and laying patterns can be chosen to suit the style of your garden and home.

Clear boundaries

Fencing & Privacy

Garden fencing can improve privacy, security and the overall look of your outdoor space. We install new fence panels, posts, gates and complete garden boundaries to suit different needs and garden layouts.

We make sure the fencing is strong, practical and built to last. Access, ground levels and durability are all considered to create a neat, reliable finish.

Planting defined

Raised Beds

Raised garden beds create neat planting areas and can make gardening easier and more comfortable. We build raised beds using timber, blockwork and other suitable materials, with practical heights and strong, tidy edges.

Each raised bed is planned to fit naturally with nearby paving, paths, lawns and planting areas, creating a clean and well-finished garden layout.
