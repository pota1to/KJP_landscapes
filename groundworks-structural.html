Groundworks & Structural

Strong outdoor spaces begin below the surface. We prepare practical bases, concrete areas and drainage systems with close attention to levels, use and the surrounding property.

Discuss your project ↗

01 · Built from the base

Concrete Work

From paths and slabs to hardstanding and dependable bases, good concrete work starts with careful preparation. We consider the intended load, surrounding levels and access before forming a suitable foundation.

The result is a practical, well-finished surface designed to sit neatly within the wider outdoor space.

02 · Water managed well

Drainage Solutions

Standing water and poor falls can limit how a garden or paved area is used. We assess the site and recommend a proportionate approach, which may include channels, pipework, corrected levels or a soakaway-style solution where appropriate.

Drainage is planned as part of the surrounding landscape so the finished surface works as one complete system.
