Built from the base

Concrete Work

From concrete paths and paving slabs to hardstanding and concrete bases, we provide strong, durable groundwork for a wide range of outdoor projects.

Every job starts with careful ground preparation to make sure the base is suitable for its intended use. We consider ground levels, access and drainage before creating a solid foundation designed for long-lasting performance and a clean, professional finish.

Water managed well

Drainage Solutions

Poor drainage can cause standing water, waterlogged lawns and damage to patios, paving and other outdoor areas. We provide professional garden drainage and ground drainage solutions.

Depending on the site, this may include drainage channels, underground pipework, correcting ground levels or installing a soakaway. Our aim is to improve water flow, protect your outdoor space and create a reliable drainage system that works with the rest of your landscaping.
